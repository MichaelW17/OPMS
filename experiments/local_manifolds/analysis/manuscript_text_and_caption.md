For each of Links A–D, we select the OPMS design with the highest validated SNR among the 1024 original 64-HG, ten-frame endpoints. Each pseudo-mode retains its optimized waist and is normalized separately to the original launch power. Two beam-parameter directions are selected using the smallest absolute SNR curvatures measured at equal first-order CSD-change scales. The directions are determined from 4800 new atmospheric realizations; 1000 additional independent realizations are reserved for validation. The complete Hessian is evaluated in 1260 real coefficient coordinates after removing frame-wise amplitude and phase redundancies, with the CSD metric used to resolve physically distinguishable directions.

Figure 5 evaluates a $9\times9$ grid over $u,v\in[-0.1,0.1]$. Maximum absolute SNR changes are 0.0177, 0.0146, 0.0085, and 0.0069 dB for Links A–D, respectively, while maximum CSD distances range from 0.139 to 0.141. Bernstein bounds evaluated using exact rational arithmetic certify a one-to-one CSD parameterization with two independent tangent directions throughout the full square for all four links. They also certify $|S(u,v)-S(0,0)|<0.05\,\mathrm{dB}$ throughout this region in the stored 1000-atmosphere response model. These results establish smooth local source manifolds accommodating continuous CSD changes with nearly constant SNR.
